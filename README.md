vCita provides an all‑in‑one small business management platform powered by AI, offering CRM, billing and invoicing, online scheduling, client portal, marketing campaigns, and AI‑driven voice and chat receptionists. It helps small businesses streamline client communication, automate lead management, and grow efficiently with integrated tools and AI assistance.

**Areas:** Company · Small Business · CRM · Scheduling · Invoicing · AI

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for vCita: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
